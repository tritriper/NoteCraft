# 2026-11-27 — US-L0-10: atomic multi-file transactions and crash recovery

- `notecraft-store`: the journal codec (`Journal`, `digest`, `is_reserved`, `journal_blob`, family
  `notecraft-journal`), `ReadOnlyReason::Recovery` with `Workspace::with_read_only`, `Workspace::sweep`
  (craft-atomic temporaries, orphan journal files, staging folders of a killed `create`; bounded, no
  links followed), and a folder flush after `remove`.
- `notecraft-engine`: `Files::journaled` (`DiskFiles` returns true); `Session::commit` plans, arms the
  journal for two or more changes, applies, and removes the head as the commit point; rollback then clear;
  a pending flag that makes the next commit recover first; `.cache/` is reserved for commands;
  `workspace.open` recovers (or refuses and opens read-only), sweeps, and returns `recovery`.
- New dependency: `sha2` (direct in the store; already in `Cargo.lock` through craft-format).
- Decisions: [ADR 0006](../adr/0006-multi-file-commit-journal.md) (accepted).
