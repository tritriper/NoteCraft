# Asset attribution

Every non-code asset in this repository (icons, images, fonts, sample and fixture files) is
listed here with its author, source and licence. `cargo xtask assets` (part of `cargo xtask ci`)
fails if an asset file has no row in this table: a file under `assets/`, or a file with an asset
extension (images, fonts, PDFs, office documents, audio, video) anywhere in the tree.

**Policy (mandatory):** NoteCraft contains **no assets taken from the reference product or from
any other proprietary product**: no icons, illustrations, templates, emoji sets, fonts, colour
themes, sample content or marketing text. Every asset is original work by NoteCraft contributors
or third-party material under a licence allowed by the Craft suite's
[licence standard](https://github.com/storytold/craft-contrib/blob/main/standards/licences.md)
and [clean-room standard](https://github.com/storytold/craft-contrib/blob/main/standards/clean-room.md).
Screenshots of other vendors' software are never committed. Font files belong in
[storytold/craft-fonts](https://github.com/storytold/craft-fonts), not here.
